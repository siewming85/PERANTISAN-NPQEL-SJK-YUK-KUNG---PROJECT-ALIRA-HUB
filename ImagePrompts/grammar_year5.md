# Grammar Comics — Year 5 (English Plus 1)

Eight comics, each **ONE image containing all 8 panels**. Generate with:

```
python generate_image.py grammar_year5
```

Approved pages go into **`komikgrammar/`**, never `koleksigambar/`.

## Scope

Extracted verbatim from the textbook's Contents spread, which has a
**LANGUAGE FOCUS** column per unit (this PDF has a real text layer; the
digits come through as ligature artefacts like `/one.fitted`, so they were
de-mangled before reading).

| Unit | Language focus in the book | Comic |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | there's / there are + a, an, some, any | — see note |
| 2 | Present simple: **affirmative and negative** | 1 |
| 3 | **Superlative adjectives** | — Year 3 covers |
| 4 | Present continuous: affirmative and negative; **continuous vs simple** | 2 |
| 5 | Countable and uncountable + **some, any, much, many, a lot of** | 3 |
| 6 | **there was / there were, was, were** | 4 |
| 6 | **Regular and irregular past verbs** | 5 |
| 7 | **Object pronouns** | 6 |
| 7 | Past simple: affirmative and **negative** | 7 |
| 8 | **be going to**: affirmative, negative and questions | 8 |

**Deliberately skipped**, since earlier years already cover them and comics
are keyed by level+topic: Unit 1's *there is/are* (Year 2) and comparatives
(Year 3), Unit 2's adverbs of frequency (Year 3), Unit 3's superlatives
(Year 3), Unit 8's *can for ability* (Years 1–3).

Where a topic *is* repeated, it is because English Plus 1 genuinely escalates
it — Year 3 taught affirmative past simple, Year 5 adds the **negative**;
Year 4 taught *going to* plans, Year 5 adds **negatives and questions**.

Captions run 6–10 words — Year 5 learners are eleven, and this is a
secondary-style course.

## The recipe

Unchanged from the Year 4 batch, which needed zero retries. All five
clauses are load-bearing: EIGHT-panels-in-capitals; the 4×2 arithmetic plus
TALL PORTRAIT; artwork filling each panel edge to edge; no sub-panels and no
repeated moments; each panel its own closed rectangle so neighbours never
merge; and **no text at all**, with emotions shown only through faces, hands
and posture — never write *nods* or *says no* in a prompt, or the model
letters it in.
