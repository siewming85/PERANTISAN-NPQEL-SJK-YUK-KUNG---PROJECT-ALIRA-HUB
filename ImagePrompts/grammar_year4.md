# Grammar Comics — Year 4 (Get Smart Plus 4)

Eight comics, each **ONE image containing all 8 panels**. Generate with:

```
python generate_image.py grammar_year4
```

Approved pages go into **`komikgrammar/`**, never `koleksigambar/`.

## Scope

Extracted verbatim from the textbook's own Contents table, which has a
**Structures** column per module (the PDF has a real text layer, so this was
read directly rather than visually):

| Module | Structures in the book | Comic |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Where are you from? | Where are you from? I'm from Mexico. I'm Mexican. | 1 |
| 2 My week | have to / don't have to · How often…? Once/twice a week | 2 |
| 3 In the past | Did you wake up at 10:00? Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. | 3 |
| 4 Celebrations | going to (future plans) · dates | 4 |
| 5 Eating right | How many…? / How much…? | 5 |
| 6 Getting around | How long does it take? What time does it leave? | 6 |
| 7 Helping out | Whose is this? It's mine/ours · someone/anything | 7 |
| 10 What's the matter? | should / shouldn't | 8 |

**Deliberately skipped**, because Year 3 already covers them and the comics
are keyed by level+topic: Module 1's adverbs of frequency and present
continuous, Module 8's comparatives, Module 9's superlatives. Year 4's own
step up on those (`more dangerous than`, `as … as`) would be worth adding
later as separate topics if wanted.

Captions run 5–9 words — longer than Year 2, these are ten-year-olds.

## The recipe

Every clause below exists because dropping it broke a page. The Year 2
batch added the last two after a page took four attempts:

1. **"EXACTLY EIGHT panels and no more"**, capitals, plus the arithmetic
   (4 rows × 2 = 8) and **"TALL PORTRAIT … do not use 4 columns and do not
   make the page landscape"**.
2. **"each panel's artwork fills its whole panel edge to edge"**.
3. **"Never divide a panel into smaller sub-panels, and never show the same
   moment twice."**
4. **"Each of the eight panels is its own separate closed rectangle; never
   let two neighbouring panels share one continuous background or merge into
   a single wide picture."** — two panels sharing a setting is what makes the
   model merge them.
5. **No text at all**, and emotions **only** through faces, hands and
   posture — a page literally rendered the word "Nod." because the prompt
   said *nods*. Never write *nods*, *says no*, or *shouts* in a prompt.
